FAQ

Questions you might have

If your question isn't here, just ask.

What does your process look like?

Usually four steps: discovery → strategy → build → iterate on data. Each stage ends with a formal check-in so the direction stays honest.

How long does a project usually take?

A full brand programme runs about 4–6 weeks, a website 2–4 weeks, and growth work is a monthly cycle. It depends on scope and how fast feedback comes back.

Can we take design only, without development?

Yes. We work either design-only or design-plus-front-end, and you can choose per stage.

Do you take smaller budgets?

Yes. What matters to us is whether the problem is clear and the goal measurable. If it's a fit, we'll propose something that suits your stage.

Is there a limit on revisions?

Each major stage includes two to three rounds of revisions. If the direction changes fundamentally, we'll flag early whether it becomes a new stage.
