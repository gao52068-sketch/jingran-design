MORII · Recovering 34%
of lost conversion

We used data to locate the leak, then ran 11 A/B tests to lift add-to-cart → payment conversion from 2.1% to 2.8%.

ClientMORII
IndustryBeauty DTC
ServicesGrowth
Timeline3 months
Background

Traffic wasn't the issue — knowing where it leaked was

MORII is a DTC beauty brand with steady monthly actives and healthy ad ROI. But 68% of users who added to cart never reached payment.

Internally there were more than 20 “I think it's here” guesses: shipping costs, payment methods, sign-up friction, the coupon field…

The problem wasn't a lack of ideas — it was not knowing which one to validate first.

What we had to solve
  • Blind spots: analytics stopped at page level, so nobody could see which interaction people were stuck on.
  • Too many hypotheses: 23 to validate, no way to prioritise, changes made on instinct.
  • Random iteration: no control group, so after launch nobody could tell whether a change worked.
Our approach

See it before you build it

For three months we did one thing: replaced guessing with testing. Every change had a hypothesis, a control and a conclusion.

01

Analytics coverage

Analytics moved from page level down to 14 key interaction points — the first clear view of where people dropped off.

02

Funnel audit

We isolated two leaks — unclear shipping costs and payment methods hidden behind a toggle — accounting for 41% of drop-off.

03

Prioritisation

We used the ICE model to cut 23 hypotheses down to six, putting resources where the impact was.

04

A/B iteration

11 tests over three months, four winners, all folded into the design guidelines and dev docs.
